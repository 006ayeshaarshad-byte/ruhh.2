About Ruhh

A safe space for people who need it the most.

What is Ruhh?

Ruhh (روح) means "soul" in Urdu. It's a platform where people can feel less alone in this vast universe. From struggles written by others in the community section, to the AI companion luna; Ruhh is here to provide a shoulder to lean on.

Our Mission

To make Pakistan a place where mental health is not a taboo topic, and people can discuss their struggles freely.

Our Vision

To create a Pakistan where platforms related to mental health are widely spread and used, for the betterment of community.

What We Offer

AI Chat

An AI companion LUNA is available whenever you need to unwind.

Journal

Private, mood-tagged journaling to help you process your thoughts.

Community

Anonymous stories from fellow peers sharing their mental health journeys.

Daily Affirmations

Curated affirmations to start your day with positivity and hope.

Helplines

Direct access to verified Pakistan mental health helplines.

Blogs

Description and research articles on most widely prevalent mental health issues found in Pakistan.
